General Medicine

What This Department Means

General Medicine deals with the prevention, diagnosis, and non-surgical treatment of adult diseases. It covers chronic health conditions like diabetes, high blood pressure, respiratory infections, seasonal viral fevers, and multi-system medical illnesses.

  • Comprehensive Medical Diagnosis & Treatment
  • Management of Diabetes, Hypertension & Fevers
  • Continuous Inpatient Medical Supervision
  • Preventive Health Checkups & Lifestyle Guidance
