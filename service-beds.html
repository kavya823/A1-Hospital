IN-DEPTH FACILITY PROFILE

Clinical Readiness & Patient Standards

At A1 Hospital, our inpatient accommodation has been architected to merge the sterile rigor of a tertiary hospital with the serene comfort of modern hospitality. Accommodating over 100 inpatients across multiple care tiers, our facility offers private luxury suites, air-conditioned twin-sharing rooms, general healing wards, and dedicated surgical recovery spaces.

Each room is fitted with motorized multi-position patient beds, continuous biometric vitals tracking, centralized high-flow oxygen supply, ergonomic attendant couches, and sterile anti-microbial surfaces. Our infection-control team enforces daily multi-stage sanitation regimens to guarantee a spotless and restful healing environment.

Motorized Ergonomic Beds

Ergonomic multi-angle adjustments allowing patients optimal posture during post-op recovery and meals.

Centralized Gas & O2

Instantaneous bedside access to oxygen and medical suction pipelines connected directly to central oxygen banks.

24/7 Vitals & Nurse Call

Rapid digital bell system connecting every bed directly to the central nursing station for prompt responses.

Attendant Comforts

Dedicated living space and sleeper sofas for loved ones accompanying the patient throughout their hospital stay.
