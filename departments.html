Nephrology (Kidney Care)

Nephrology is the medical specialty that deals with kidney diseases, kidney damage prevention, fluid imbalances, and dialysis care. It focuses on keeping kidneys functioning and managing conditions caused by diabetes or high blood pressure.

Urology

Urology focuses on the medical and surgical management of the urinary tract (kidneys, ureters, bladder, and urethra) in men and women, as well as male reproductive health. Common treatments include kidney stone management and prostate care.

Ophthalmology (Eye Care)

Ophthalmology is the branch of medicine and surgery that treats disorders of the eye and vision. It includes comprehensive eyesight testing, cataract evaluation, glaucoma screening, and medical treatment for ocular infections and allergies.
