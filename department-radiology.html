CLINICAL SCOPE & CARE

Specialized Medical Care & Procedures

Prompt and unambiguous medical imaging is the backbone of clinical decision-making. Headed by Dr. G. Sandeep (M.D RADIOLOGY), the Department of Radiology at A1 Hospital delivers high-clarity diagnostic scans across all clinical specialties.

Our digital CT scanner captures razor-sharp anatomical cross-sections for immediate stroke triage, polytrauma evaluation, and abdominal diagnosis. In maternal care, our 4D high-definition ultrasound provides comprehensive fetal anomaly evaluations and color Doppler scans.

Digital Multi-Slice CT

Instantaneous non-invasive visualization of brain, thorax, abdomen, and skeletal structures.

4D Obstetric Ultrasound

Detailed high-resolution fetal morphology scans detecting congenital anomalies early in gestation.

Vascular Color Doppler

Non-invasive assessment of carotid, arterial, and deep venous circulation to detect blood clots.

High-Frequency Digital X-Ray

Low-radiation digital radiography for accurate skeletal fracture detection and chest pathology.
